# Organic CTR and Search Growth Roadmap

## Objective

Turn existing Google impressions into qualified consultation clicks without publishing invented prices, medical guarantees, or thin AI pages.

## Priority query clusters

| Priority | Search intent | Primary page | Recommended title direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | dental implant prices in Cyprus | `/en/implants` plus a clinician-reviewed cost section | Dental Implant Prices in Cyprus: Cost Factors & Personal Quotes |
| 1 | all-on-4 dental implant cost Cyprus | `/en/all-on-4` | All-on-4 Dental Implant Cost in Cyprus: What Is Included? |
| 1 | all-on-6 dental implant cost Cyprus | New CMS treatment/guide after clinical approval | All-on-6 Dental Implant Cost in Cyprus: Treatment Guide |
| 2 | dental implants abroad prices | Implant cost comparison guide | Dental Implants Abroad: Costs, Safety and What to Compare |

## Content requirements before price pages are published

- Clinic-approved price ranges and currency.
- A precise list of what the quote includes and excludes.
- Implant system/brand choices without unsupported superiority claims.
- Number of visits, healing stages and common factors that change cost.
- Named clinical reviewer and last-reviewed date.
- Clear statement that suitability and final price require examination and imaging.

## CTR improvements

1. Match the exact query intent in the title and opening paragraph.
2. Use a unique meta description that states the page's practical value and avoids generic superlatives.
3. Put the answer summary, inclusions and quote CTA above the fold.
4. Add visible FAQ answers for price, visits, implant brand, aftercare and guarantees.
5. Add relevant high-resolution clinical imagery and descriptive alt text.
6. Link cost-intent articles to the matching treatment page and the treatment page back to the guide.
7. Keep titles concise; test one material change per URL every 28 days.

## Measurement cadence

- Weekly: query impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by landing page and language.
- Every 28 days: rewrite titles/descriptions only for URLs with meaningful impressions and below-baseline CTR.
- Monthly: inspect mobile snippets, rich-result eligibility, Core Web Vitals and indexed-page coverage.
- Quarterly: consolidate cannibalizing articles and refresh clinical review dates.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish a numerical price until the clinic confirms it.
- Do not auto-publish medical AI translations; require editorial review.
- Do not create localized URLs whose primary content remains English; keep them `noindex` until translated.
- Do not promise guaranteed outcomes, painless treatment, fixed longevity or universal eligibility.
